# OpenAI secure use

Working guidance on confidentiality and know-how protection when using ChatGPT, Codex and the OpenAI API.

## Product vision

Organizations need to decide when AI assistance is worth exposing confidential
information or business know-how to an external service. Business owners,
technology leaders, security teams and legal advisers need a shared basis for
understanding the information at risk, the conditions of processing, the
available safeguards and the uncertainty that remains.

Provide a practical guide that helps those readers decide
what information may be used with ChatGPT, Codex and the OpenAI API, for which
tasks, through which approved environments, under whose authority and with
what required evidence. The product is the guidance document. An adopting
organization implements, tests and approves its own controls.

Small organizations need approaches they can operate with limited specialist
support. Medium-sized organizations need coordinated rules and responsibilities
across teams. Large organizations need consistent oversight across business
units and varied environments. Responsibilities may be combined, but information
sensitivity and applicable obligations still determine the protection needed.

Success means a reader can take a proposed use and explain its business value,
the information at risk, the permitted service and access route, the accountable
decision makers, the required safeguards and evidence, and the remaining risk.
The guide must also help the reader recognize when evidence is insufficient
and sensitive information should be withheld.

This repository covers the confidentiality and know-how domain of AI governance for OpenAI use. It supports information-use and access decisions; an organization-wide AI inventory is an input. It does not constitute a complete AI governance framework or establish compliance or certification. Confidentiality approval does not replace quality, fairness, output-rights or agent-action reviews.

## Standards and regulatory mapping

[Standards and regulatory mapping](appendices/en/appendix-a-standards-and-regulatory-mapping.md)

The mapping connects ISO/IEC 42001:2023 management clauses, selected NIST AI RMF 1.0 outcomes and applicable EU AI Act provisions to the chapters, evidence requirements and gaps. Legal applicability depends on scope, role, intended use and timing. It is not a certification or compliance declaration.
