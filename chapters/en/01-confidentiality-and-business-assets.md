## 1. Confidentiality and Business Assets

Confidentiality concerns more than personal data or the loss of a file. A business may retain its original documents yet lose an advantage if its unpublished methods, pricing logic or research become available to another party. Protection must therefore consider both unauthorized disclosure and unauthorized reuse of business knowledge.
