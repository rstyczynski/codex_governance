## 7. Accounts, Authentication and Service Boundaries

The same client can be used under different commercial and administrative arrangements. A company must identify the account, workspace and authentication route actually used, rather than infer protection from the application name or the fact that a subscription is paid.

**Chapter sketch**

- Compare personal ChatGPT accounts, company Business or Enterprise workspaces and OpenAI API projects.
- Distinguish Codex sign-in through ChatGPT from API-key access and record the resulting contractual and administrative scope.
- Assess CLI, IDE, desktop and cloud workflows separately; do not assume gateway support transfers between them.
- Establish company identity, account provisioning, offboarding and restrictions on personal accounts.
- Record product versions and features required for each approved route.

OpenAI documents both ChatGPT and API-key authentication for local Codex work, with the sign-in method affecting applicable controls and data-handling policies. [OpenAI authentication documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/auth).

**Planned output:** An approved-services register containing client, account type, workspace or project identifier, authentication method and owner.
